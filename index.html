About Conixstudio

Visual stories with purpose.

Conixstudio is a creative photography studio led by Collins Nixon, focused on capturing people, moments and stories through bold, timeless imagery.

From studio portraits and outdoor sessions to conceptual work, documentary photography and weddings, every shoot is approached with intention, creativity and attention to detail.

The goal is simple — create photographs that don't just look good, but make you remember how the moment felt.
